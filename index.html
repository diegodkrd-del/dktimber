Direct-From-Mill Hardwood Sourcing

Buying timber without expert technical oversight often leads to high scrap rates, warp, and inflated costs. We bridge the gap between South American forestry mills and North American buyers to ensure structural integrity and maximum yield.

Precision Application Matching

Every construction project requires specific Janka hardness values, bending strength (MOR), and natural rot resistance. We match your architectural requirements to the exact South American hardwood species that meets specifications without over-budgeting.

Eliminating Middleman Costs

Standard lumber import chains pass through multiple regional brokers, importers, and distributors before hitting your yard. We negotiate container-load rates straight from partner mills, cutting intermediate markups by up to 30%.

Rigorous Quality Auditing

Our team conducts physical on-site inspections prior to container loading. We verify moisture content thresholds, grade compliance, precise board dimensions, and proper kiln drying techniques.

How Our Sourcing Process Works

From initial structural specifications to direct yard delivery, our end-to-end management keeps your projects fully stocked and within budget.

01

Project Consultation

We analyze your build requirements, structural loads, weather exposure, and aesthetic targets to determine the ideal timber profile.

02

Direct Mill Selection

We negotiate container-load supply directly with certified South American sawmills, securing volume discounts and strict grading guarantees.

03

On-Site Quality Control

Before loading, every batch undergoes hands-on inspection for moisture content, core integrity, square cuts, and sustainable harvesting compliance.

04

Logistics & Yard Delivery

We manage all customs clearances, port tariffs, and flatbed transport directly to your job site or regional distribution yard.
